Claude AI Export

Take your Claude data with you.

  1. We open claude.ai in a background tab, using the account you are already signed in to.
  2. We read your data — chats and their full history, projects, attachments, scheduled tasks.
  3. You get a .zip in your Downloads folder. That file is the whole export.

Everything happens inside this browser. Your conversations are never sent to us or to anyone else — there is nowhere for them to go.

Open source — read every line

No trackers, no accounts, no runtime dependencies. The bundles here are built from that source and nothing else — npm run verify rebuilds them and fails if a byte differs. The only permission this extension holds is to talk to claude.ai.
